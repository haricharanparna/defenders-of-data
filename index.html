Why It Matters

Teens spend hours online every day, and scammers know it. One bad click can leak your passwords, your photos or your location.

  • $12.5 billion was lost to fraud reported to the U.S. FTC in 2024. Source: FTC
  • Phishing (fake messages and links) is the most reported type of cybercrime to the FBI. Source: FBI IC3 2024 report
  • 2-step verification blocks most automated attempts to break into your accounts. Source: Google research
