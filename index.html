How to Play

  1. 🐛
    Threats are coming. Phishers, password crackers and malware travel down the path toward your Data Core.
  2. 🛡
    Build defenses. Pick a tower, then click an empty spot next to the path to place it.
  3. 🎯
    Match the threat. Each tower is a real safety habit, and it works best against certain threats. Choose wisely!
  4. 🏆
    Survive every wave to protect your data and win the level.

Why It Matters

Teens spend hours online every day, and scammers know it. One bad click can leak your passwords, your photos or your location.

  • $12.5 billion was lost to fraud reported to the U.S. FTC in 2024.
  • Phishing (fake messages and links) is the most reported type of cybercrime to the FBI.
  • 2-step verification blocks most automated attempts to break into your accounts.

Every tower in this game is a real habit you can use today. Defend the game, then defend yourself!
