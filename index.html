How to Play

  1. 🔓
    Unlock your defenses. Every tower is a real safety habit. Pass its quick challenge to unlock it.
  2. 🛡️
    Build towers. Pick a tower, then click an empty spot next to the path to place it.
  3. 📩
    Scan the threats. Threats arrive as mystery messages. Click one, read it and decide what kind of threat it is. Get it right and the matching tower does double damage!
  4. ✅
    Real or Scam? Between waves, spot which messages are real and which are scams to earn bytes for more towers.
  5. 💬
    Answer your DMs. Messages pop up mid-wave. Pick the safe reply fast for an Overclock boost. A risky reply lets more threats in!
  6. ⭐
    Upgrade and blast. Click a tower to upgrade it with a harder question. Right answers in a row charge your 💥 Firewall Blast.
  7. 👾
    Survive forever. The waves never end and get tougher. Every 10th wave, the Ransomware Boss locks your towers. Answer its questions to restore them. Beat your best and earn 🏅 badges!
  8. 🗺️
    Skip ahead. Open the Level Map and pass a quick Duolingo-style quiz to jump to any wave. Remember your practice password, you'll need it for surprise login checks!

Why It Matters

Teens spend hours online every day, and scammers know it. One bad click can leak your passwords, your photos or your location.

  • $12.5 billion was lost to fraud reported to the U.S. FTC in 2024.
  • Phishing (fake messages and links) is the most reported type of cybercrime to the FBI.
  • 2-step verification blocks most automated attempts to break into your accounts.

Every tower in this game is a real habit you can use today. Defend the game, then defend yourself!
