Internet Adventure

  1. Walk with the arrow keys, WASD or the buttons. Hold Shift (or turn on 🏃 Sprint) to run fast until your stamina bar runs out. It refills when you stop. Press Space (or A) to talk and read signs.
  2. Wild threats hide in the darker spam grass. Walk through it to find them.
  3. In battle, read each attack and pick the defense that beats it. The right one is super effective!
  4. Quarantine beaten threats: get 7 questions right in 30 seconds and they go into your 📖 Threat Index. Too slow, and they escape!
  5. 60 threats live in 6 areas, 10 in each. Rarer ones (Rare, Epic, Legendary and the super-tough Mythic) are harder to find, beat and quarantine.
  6. Beat the hacker trainers to earn badges, open the gates and get new 🦉 guardians that fight beside you in boss battles. Omega Ransomware waits at the very end.
